SECTION 4: WARRANTY AND RETURNS POLICIES
========================================================

Policy REF-2024-009: Warranty and Returns Procedure
Effective Date: 1 March 2024
Supersedes: REF-2021-003

4.1  Standard Warranty
     All Acme Robotics products carry a 24-month warranty from the date of purchase.
     Warranty covers manufacturing defects and component failure under normal use.
     Physical damage, misuse, and third-party modifications are excluded.

4.2  Return Window
     Customers wishing to return goods that are not faulty may do so within
     14 calendar days of receipt, provided the item is unused and in its original
     packaging. Returns outside this window will not be accepted.

4.3  Returns Procedure (staff must follow these steps in order):
     Step 1 — Customer contacts Sandra Okafor (returns@acme-robotics.co.uk, ext 4421)
              to obtain a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number.
     Step 2 — Sandra issues the RMA via the CRM system within 2 working days.
     Step 3 — Customer ships the item with the RMA number clearly marked on the parcel.
     Step 4 — Goods Inward team inspects the unit within 3 working days of arrival.
     Step 5 — Sandra approves or rejects the return and notifies the customer by email.
     Step 6 — Finance (Colette Bramwell, ext 6630) processes refund within 5 working days.

4.4  Complaint Response SLA
     All customer complaints submitted to complaints@acme-robotics.co.uk must receive
     an initial response within 3 working days. Escalations are handled by the
     relevant department head.

SECTION 5: REMOTE WORK POLICY
========================================================

Policy REF-2023-041: Remote and Hybrid Working
Effective Date: 15 September 2023

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week, subject to manager
approval. All remote working must be carried out from a secure, private location.
Employees are responsible for ensuring their home internet connection is adequate.

Engineering and production-floor roles are excluded from remote working eligibility.
Queries regarding remote working eligibility should be directed to your line manager.

SECTION 6: ANNUAL LEAVE AND ABSENCE
========================================================

6.1  Annual Leave Entitlement
     All full-time employees are entitled to 28 days of paid annual leave per year,
     inclusive of UK bank holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement.

6.2  Requesting Leave
     Step 1 — Submit a leave request via the HR portal (hr.acme-robotics.internal)
              at least 5 working days in advance for up to 3 consecutive days.
     Step 2 — Requests for 4 or more consecutive days must be submitted 15 working
              days in advance.
     Step 3 — Your line manager will approve or decline within 3 working days.
     Step 4 — Approved leave is automatically added to the HR system calendar.

6.3  Probationary Period
     New employees are subject to a 6-month probationary period. During this period,
     notice on both sides is 2 weeks. Employees on probation may not carry over
     unused annual leave.

SECTION 7: EXPENSES POLICY
========================================================

Policy REF-2024-015: Staff Expenses
Effective Date: 12 May 2024

7.1  Submission Deadline
     All expense claims must be submitted by the last working day of each month
     for the expenses incurred in that calendar month. Claims submitted late will
     be processed in the following month's payment run.

7.2  Receipt Requirements
     Receipts are required for all expenses of £25 or more. Expenses below £25
     may be claimed without a receipt, but a brief written description is still required.

7.3  Approval
     Expenses up to £500 are approved by the employee's direct line manager.
     Expenses between £500 and £2,000 require sign-off from the relevant department head.
     Expenses above £2,000 require approval from the CFO (Beatrice Holloway).

7.4  Mileage
     Business mileage is reimbursed at HMRC's approved rate of 45p per mile for
     the first 10,000 miles per tax year, and 25p per mile thereafter.

SECTION 8: IT AND DATA SECURITY POLICY
========================================================

8.1  Password Policy
     All staff passwords must be a minimum of 12 characters and include at least
     one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
     Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Do not reuse any of your last 10 passwords.

8.2  Data Retention
     Customer and transaction records must be retained for a minimum of 7 years
     in compliance with HMRC requirements and UK GDPR obligations.
     Records older than 7 years must be securely destroyed in line with
     Policy REF-2024-015 (Data Retention Addendum, Appendix C).

8.3  Health and Safety Inspections
     Priya Subramaniam (Health & Safety Officer, ext 5517) conducts formal safety
     inspections of all Acme Robotics premises quarterly (every 3 months).
     All staff are required to cooperate fully with inspection procedures.

8.4  Staff ID Badges
     Staff ID badges must be worn visibly at all times on company premises.
     Badges are renewed every 2 years. Lost badges must be reported to reception
     within 1 working day and replaced within 3 working days at a cost of £15.

SECTION 9: GRIEVANCE AND DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES
========================================================

Acme Robotics follows a three-stage disciplinary process:
  Stage 1 — Verbal warning (recorded in HR file)
  Stage 2 — Written warning (valid for 12 months)
  Stage 3 — Final written warning or dismissal

Employees wishing to raise a formal grievance should submit a written statement
to the HR department at hr@acme-robotics.co.uk within 10 working days of the
incident in question.
